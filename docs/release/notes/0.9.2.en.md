**Maintafox 0.9.2: test version, for internal test PCs only**

This update fixes what the hands-on test of 0.9.1 and a full review of every screen and workflow found. Please still don't use it with real plant data.

**Before installing**
- Upgrade all the PCs of a site together. A PC on 0.9.2 refuses some changes sent by a PC still on 0.9.1 (for example a password reset or a delegation policy sent without its author) until that PC is upgraded. Nothing is lost: the change waits.

**Sign-in and users**
- After an update, the dashboard and the menu come back complete. You no longer get "Session expirée" with only the overview and settings in the menu.
- The activation screen is now a compact window with three steps (licence key, connection, administrator). A refused licence reset says why and what to do. Technical details appear once. The password field shows one eye icon, not two.
- A PC left alone now really locks after the idle time. Too many wrong passwords on the lock screen or on a password prompt now lock the account, as at sign-in.
- A second PC joins the plant first and does not offer to create a first administrator.
- The role editor no longer lists vendor-console or not-yet-delivered module rights, and each right has a French and English name. An administrator can no longer reset their own password by mistake and lock themselves out.
- A refused action now shows a clear message instead of covering the whole screen. Buttons are offered only to users who may use them.
- A right changed on another PC now takes effect here without signing in again. Sign-in and unlock are refused while an update is installing, with a clear message.

**Work requests and work orders**
- The work-order board no longer shows "Fermé" and "Annulé" lanes twice: closed and cancelled orders are in the archive only, as for requests.
- A mandatory task can no longer be counted as done when it is marked cancelled or postponed. Task results can no longer be changed once the order is closed or cancelled.
- Cancelling an order in progress now asks for the password and completes. The close-out refusal reads as a clear list of what is missing.
- Two people entering close-out coding, root cause or service cost no longer silently overwrite each other.
- No work order or request can be raised on retired equipment, including from an inspection anomaly.
- Approving an integrity waiver now needs a second person who is really eligible, and the list offers only those people.

**Permits and lockout (LOTO)**
- A permit required by a safety request can no longer be unticked by whoever edits the order. Plant permit rules now apply to every work order, not only converted requests.
- An order cannot be closed or cancelled while its permit is still active or locks are still fitted. A permit cannot be closed or cancelled while its work is in progress.
- If a permit was ended on another PC before work started here, the conflict is now flagged instead of passing silently.

**Planning and preventive maintenance**
- A break-in into a frozen week now needs the named approver's password, and the planner cannot approve themselves. Work arriving from another PC into a frozen week is checked too.
- Preventive plan versions numbered alike on two PCs are both kept. The planner is warned if one due date is planned twice. A failed move to "Prêt à planifier" no longer leaves an orphan work order.
- The planning PDF export is in the app language, with local dates and times.
- Reassigning an order, or changing a crew's site, can no longer push committed jobs into another site's frozen week without a break-in. The refusal says which week. A signed break-in always leaves its journal entry.

**Stock and inventory counts**
- The same count posted on two PCs no longer applies the stock adjustment twice. A count can no longer read "posted" without moving stock.
- Issuing the same reservation, or receiving the same order line, on two PCs no longer drives reserved stock negative unnoticed. The surplus is flagged for review.
- A goods receipt takes the article from the order line. Editing a count line no longer clears its approval silently.

**Equipment and reliability**
- The equipment picker now offers your recent and most-used equipment as soon as you click, and the full list when the plant has 50 or fewer. In the planning-window dialog it has the same height as the other fields.
- Equipment moves are checked the same way everywhere. An approved reliability decision can no longer create two work orders, and cannot be bypassed.
- Analytics filtered on a site now count its sub-sites. Finance figures are hidden from users without the finance right.
- Moving equipment from its edit form now records a "Déplacé" event (who, when, from where, to where), and it reaches the other PCs. Notices about retired equipment follow the order's current site.
- Each indicator now shows its "stable" band in its definition. Preventive work due today or within 7 days is no longer shown as bad; only overdue work is red.

**Synchronisation between PCs**
- The Sync Centre shows the real reason of each conflict and what each waiting record is waiting for.
- Decisions, break-ins and return-to-service checks can no longer be deleted or rewritten by synchronisation. Settings that belong to one PC are no longer overwritten by another.

**Messages, dates and languages**
- The app opens in French, even on an English Windows. Refusals, journal entries and many labels that appeared in English or as raw codes are now in the app language.
- Dates show as dd/mm/yyyy with a 24-hour clock, and the calendar icon is no longer cut off. Decimals follow the French format in planning, work orders and attachments.
- Password rules are shown in the user's language, one line per broken rule. The refusal to resolve a sync conflict without a reason is translated too.
- "Quoi de neuf" in the update banner is collapsed and shows clean text, without stray symbols. The login screen shows a translated licence level.

Please report anything that looks wrong, with the screen and the time it happened.
