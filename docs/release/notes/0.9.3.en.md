**Maintafox 0.9.3: test version, for internal test PCs only**

This update fixes what the full hands-on test of 0.9.2 by a newcomer to the software found, screen by screen and workflow by workflow. Please still don't use it with real plant data.

**Before installing**
- Upgrade all the PCs of a site the same day, after the sync server has been updated (the administrator or the vendor does the server first). A PC still on 0.9.2 cannot send the new kinds of records: permit gas tests, isolation points, personal padlocks, 5-why analyses, corrective actions, imported work history and others.
- After the upgrade, each user signs in once on each PC they use. All their changes are signed in their name; a change waits on that PC until they sign in there. A PC still on 0.9.2 can no longer send most changes (work orders, requests, permits, stock, accounts, rights…).
- A change that names who did, decided or approved something must now be signed by that person, and the sync server checks it. A site's four-eyes opt-out and a permit type's three-person padlock-removal setting can no longer be changed in someone else's name, and a reliability decision counts as approved only with the approver's own signed approval. History records can no longer be rewritten, and a permit type keeps its code. A decision approval received from another PC is applied only once the approver's signed approval record has arrived: approvals made on 0.9.1 or earlier wait. Not checked yet: who may delete a photo or attachment through sync. Every such delete is kept and the vendor can restore it.
- After the upgrade, an administrator must grant two new rights. "Approve permits for HSE" ("Approuver les permis côté HSE") goes to the HSE approvers: without it, permit issuers can no longer give the HSE approval. "Place / remove my padlock" ("Poser / retirer mon cadenas") goes to the workers who place their personal padlocks.
- Reprint the equipment QR labels printed before this version. If the app says "Étiquette à réimprimer" (Label to reprint), type the asset code printed on the label, then reprint the label from the asset record.

**Planners (planning, preventive maintenance)**
- The planning backlog now matches the open work orders that are ready, and drag and drop works. Every greyed planning tool says what is missing. There is one consistent way to freeze a week.
- The planning screen no longer contradicts itself: one weekend definition, the same teams everywhere, and the freeze state follows the date. Workload is shown by trade over three weeks. The OT without an estimate are named, and teams show readable names.
- A preventive plan can replace the lower-frequency plans on the same equipment, so two OT are no longer raised the same week on one machine. A non-conforming preventive step creates a linked corrective OT in one click.
- Preventive plan states read as clear actions. A short note explains what a new plan version does to the OT already generated. "Nouveau plan" has one equipment field. "Dupliquer ce plan" copies a plan as a new draft.
- Rounds are faster to build: copy, several points at once, units, and one guided flow to create the round and its schedule. Each round measurement point has a trend curve with its limits. In the field, a skipped round point needs a reason, finishing a round asks for confirmation, and the sync banner and counts are honest.
- SLA rules have French names, an honest active state and a visible escalation.

**Technicians (work orders, requests, close-out)**
- A technician can book hours on an OT: he is preselected as himself, the rate shows before saving, and the default time is no longer fixed. Staff lists can be filtered by team.
- An OT whose end is before its start is refused with a clear message, on creation and on edit. Impossible durations are warned. OT list, board, archive and search give the same counts, find every matching OT, and show a new OT at once.
- The new "Dépannage" screen creates and starts a breakdown OT in a few clicks, with a breakdown start time. A breakdown can be validated after the fact, and a technician can join an OT already in progress or on hold. A breakdown prepares permit drafts for the required permit types when the user may issue permits.
- "Créer l'ordre de travail" starts with an empty "Choisir une urgence" and a sentence names the missing field when it is greyed. Equipment search finds several words, codes and partial names.
- Routine assignment has its own "Affecter" action. Emergency reassignment stays for real exceptions. A request can be rejected with a reason, and the disposition list comes from reference data.
- Duplicate requests can be merged into one OT, and the operator is warned while typing. The request form is short: equipment, description, photo and "Machine à l'arrêt ?". The observation date is prefilled.
- The request list is sorted by urgency then criticality, with a criticality column and one priority scale. The requester has a simple "where is my request" view and is told when it is closed.
- Approving a simple request takes fewer confirmations, and the dialog titles match the step. The same person can no longer declare, approve and convert a request. Separation of duties also applies to work orders. A safety flag on a request raises its priority and alerts the HSE approver.
- The OT parts plan shows the available quantity of each part. A purchase request can be raised from the OT sheet. Waiting reasons are typed.
- The close-out checklist is at the top and links to each missing point. Remedy and component fields were added, in plain wording. OT buttons follow the state: no "Annuler l'OT" on a finished order, distinct labels, and a warning if parts were already issued. A draft OT shows its current equipment when edited.
- A repair under warranty can be flagged on the OT with a charge-back note to the supplier. Breakdown downtime flowing into MTTR, supervisor validation, late time entry and the refusal to close without hours were checked and corrected.

**Storekeepers (stock, purchasing, contractors)**
- The extra English "MAIN" store is gone. Reorder recommendations are computed per real store and name the store. Purchasing is reachable at the top level, and the menu name matches the screen.
- Approval ceilings have defaults, approving is separate from managing purchases, and the approver is named. A direct purchase line in free text can be charged to an OT without a catalogue item.
- A non-conforming part can be returned to the supplier from its reception. Each article has a "Utilisé sur" tab listing the machines that use it.
- Returning an unused part to its OT has a visible button. Only a user with the stock right moves stock from an OT: for anyone else the part is recorded "à sortir" and the storekeeper issues it.
- The repairables list explains a missing right, and serial numbers are followed through the repair. Contractors are easy to find. An OT names its external company and contract, and contractor hours and cost can be added on the OT.
- A contract has an amount, a consumed value and an expiry alert. The create form has the same fields as the edit form, and each contractor gets a performance score. Document numbers are sequential and readable. "Approuver" is greyed on your own request, with the reason.
- Suppliers have a price history. The item form is grouped and its terms are explained. A dormant-stock view shows items with no movement. The issue counter lists only relevant OT, shows clearer messages and offers relevant units. An after-hours issue is flagged "to regularise".

**HSE (permits, LOTO, training and qualifications)**
- "Formations et habilitations" opens for the administrator, is in the side menu, and certifications can be entered with an expiry date.
- A permit has a work description, a risk assessment, preventive measures and PPE, checked before issue. HSE approval is given by a second person, never by the author or the submitter.
- Gas tests (O2, LEL, H2S) are recorded with their time, and are required for confined space. A permit lists its workers and companies with their induction. Each worker places and removes his own personal padlock on the group lock box. A permit refuses a worker whose qualification has expired.
- The permit is chosen from the OT, French labels replace raw codes, and "work at height" is a new permit type. An expired permit turns "expiré" by itself, raises an alert, and the open-permits tile is correct.
- The "Mainlevées à temps" figure is corrected and its sentence is clear. A machine that requires a permit now reminds the user: required permit types are ticked automatically.
- Isolation points are stored per equipment and proposed on the permit. The zero-energy check needs an explicit confirmation with a comment.
- Permit step buttons are greyed with the reason when you lack the right, and the steps have clearer names. Cancelling an active permit asks for a reason. The padlock shows the time it was placed. The LOTO sheet is one printable sheet per permit, with real names.

**Equipment and reliability**
- A wrong meter reading can be cancelled or corrected with a reason. An abnormal jump is warned, and a "Relevé par" column shows the author. The meter form defaults to the current time, its unit follows the meter type, and "Remplacer le compteur" exists. A meter can have alarm thresholds that raise a request or an OT.
- Each equipment family has its own technical characteristics, and the family is filled for every machine. A serial-tracked component can be moved between machines, keeping its history of fitted and removed dates.
- A dedicated "Transférer" action moves equipment to another site and records who, when, why and where. A missing required field on a new asset scrolls to and highlights the error.
- A new asset shows "—" instead of an invented health score, and its age counts from commissioning. Repeat failures are flagged on the asset, the list and the dashboard. 5-why analyses and corrective actions with an owner and a due date are tracked.
- Asset creation takes warranty, purchase date and cost. The criticality class is shown once, with its link to priority. The decommission dialog links to the blocking OT and its confirm button is disabled while blocked.
- RAMS and Analytique now give the same figures for the same period and the same data quality. The RAMS home shows tiles for lifetime analysis (Weibull), AMDEC, causes tree (Ishikawa), RCM decision and saved studies. "Analyser cet équipement" is on the asset record.
- Studies can be named, renamed, searched, printed and exported. The optimal replacement interval agrees with the Weibull reading. The RCM questions are in plain words with no unsafe default, and a critical mode is never recommended "run to failure".
- A decision needs a justification, and shows its currency and its approver. A study refuses mixed equipment classes. The ranking table sorts and opens rows. A RAMS figure opens the OT behind it.
- The Ishikawa diagram is readable on opening, and "Promouvoir en action corrective" is a visible button linked both ways with the OT. Reliability signals can be recalculated from RAMS, with the "calculé le" date. An availability trend and top-10 views were added.
- A technician no longer sees RAMS write buttons that would be refused. The asset profile shows the MTBF and a visible link to RAMS. A simple summary explains why a line's availability drops.

**Dashboards, analytics, budget and documents**
- Cost per production line and per work type (preventive, corrective, improvement) can be filtered and exported. Analytique offers calendar month, year or a free period.
- A small count change shows in units, never as a red "+300 %". The overview counts open OT one way only. The overview opens with the key figures and better default indicators, and its tiles are clickable.
- "Respect du planning" gives a number, and MP compliance is split by site and by trade. The report generator says honestly what it does and what it does not.
- A budget draft no longer replaces the reference version before approval. Two versions can be compared side by side. A gap analysis can be started from an overrun line.
- Month closing blocks future months, closing buttons have distinct names, and a reversal entry button exists. Budget Pilotage figures are labelled with a chart scale. Budget lines are grouped by cost centre and show month names.
- Equipment documents are visible on the OT. Documents have numbers, revisions, validity dates and categories, and a new version keeps the history. Search covers the whole library.
- The Archives page says what is archived and how to restore it. Retention rules have a Save button and warn before data is lost.
- Main lists (OT, requests, equipment, stock, permits) export to Excel/CSV. Equipment import accepts ";"-separated CSV, offers a template and names each missing field. Parent, location and family are given by code.
- A single "Imports de données" screen also imports the organisation tree, cost centres, crews, skills and certificates, meters, stores, suppliers, stock articles, opening stock, MP plans and past maintenance history. Imported history counts in RAMS and costs.

**Administration and sign-in**
- A site, workshop or line can be created, and the draft and publish steps of the organisation are explained. Shipped roles have French names. Magasinier, Acheteur and Responsable HSE roles are ready-made.
- The role editor has a sticky save bar, warns about unsaved changes and adds the linked rights automatically. Giving a site-limited role offers to replace the plant-wide role, and scopes have readable names.
- Actions you may not do are hidden or disabled, with the reason. Synchronised OT changes are re-checked against the rights held now. The audit can be searched by OT number and shows old and new values, in French.
- Sync conflicts are readable, side by side, with explained actions. Reference lists are grouped by domain, and the class, family and sub-family link is explained.
- French failure modes per equipment family and a list of remedies were added. Disabling a reference value asks for confirmation, the code is proposed from the label, and raw English codes are gone.
- Personnel form fixes: no error toast, one list of positions, and a readable analysis. An account without a personnel record is told up front, with an easy link.
- User creation lists only persons not yet linked. The users screen is clearer, and menus and the home page follow the user's role. Sign-in messages give the remaining attempts and how to reach an administrator. Sign-in is one window, not a window inside a window.
- Settings wording is plain French. Notification settings are grouped. A warning appears when the company print header is empty. The document header saves normally, and a refusal says exactly why, once.
- Backups can be copied outside the PC. A warning shows when none is set, and a restore guide explains moving to another PC.

**Messages, dates and languages**
- Developer text is gone from the OT "Intégrité des données" panel. Dates and times show locally as dd/mm/yyyy hh:mm everywhere.
- A visible "← Retour" returns to the previous screen on more detail screens, keeping filters. Main actions sit in a sticky bar, drop-downs are no longer cut off, and a warning asks before losing unsaved input.
- A refusal names the missing right and what to do. A disabled button says what is missing. An in-app help gives short guides per module. The document library is now "Bibliothèque documentaire".
- The bell shows business alerts, such as late requests, and groups sync conflicts. The header says what is saved and what to do. Important actions are visible: purchase request from an OT, equipment edit and decommission.
- Remaining English text, English month and day names and raw codes are now French. One vocabulary is used (MP, DA). Saving or creating shows a visible confirmation with the new item's number.
- "Annuler" in a small dialog no longer closes the sheet underneath. The "second administrator" banner no longer repeats on every page. The side menu folds on narrow windows.
- Language and dark mode are in the user menu. Ctrl K also finds people, equipment, permits and stock items.

Please report anything that looks wrong, with the screen and the time it happened.
